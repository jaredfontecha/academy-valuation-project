# Academy Sports + Outdoors: Company Overview

## Basic Information

- **Company:** Academy Sports and Outdoors, Inc.
- **Ticker:** ASO
- **Stock exchange:** Nasdaq
- **Headquarters:** Katy, Texas
- **Industry:** Sporting goods and outdoor recreation retail
- **Fiscal year end:** Saturday closest to January 31
- **2025 fiscal year end:** January 31, 2026
- **Stores:** 322 stores across 21 states
- **Mission:** “Fun for All”

## Business Description

Academy Sports + Outdoors is a full-line sporting goods and outdoor recreation retailer. The company sells products through physical stores and its e-commerce platform.

Academy targets active individuals and families by offering national brands, private-label products, and merchandise at different price levels.

## Main Product Divisions

1. Outdoors
2. Sports and Recreation
3. Apparel
4. Footwear

## 2025 Sales Mix

- Outdoors: 31%
- Sports and Recreation: 22%
- Apparel: 27%
- Footwear: 20%

## Product and Brand Strategy

Approximately 78% of Academy’s 2025 merchandise sales came from national brands. The remaining 22% came from the company’s private-label brands.

National brands include Nike, Jordan, Under Armour, Adidas, Columbia, The North Face, Yeti, Stanley, and Carhartt.

Academy also operates 19 private-label brands, including Magellan Outdoors, BCG, O’rageous, Game Winner, and Outdoor Gourmet.

## How the Company Makes Money

Academy purchases sporting goods, outdoor products, clothing, and footwear from suppliers and sells them to customers at a higher price.

The company generates revenue through:

- Physical retail stores
- Online purchases shipped to customers
- Buy online, pick up in store
- National-brand products
- Higher-margin private-label products

Academy’s profitability depends on selling enough merchandise while controlling product costs, employee expenses, rent, shipping, and other operating costs.

## Important Business Drivers

The main factors that affect Academy’s financial performance include:

- Number of stores
- Sales generated by existing stores
- New-store openings
- Online sales
- Product prices
- Number of products sold
- Gross margin
- Inventory levels
- Customer demand
- Seasonal shopping periods

## Growth Opportunities

Academy’s primary growth opportunities include:

- Opening stores in existing and new markets
- Expanding its presence outside its traditional southern markets
- Increasing e-commerce and buy-online-pickup-in-store sales
- Improving brand awareness and customer loyalty
- Increasing sales of private-label products
- Using technology to improve shopping and fulfillment
- Improving sales generated by existing stores

Academy opened 24 new stores during fiscal 2025. New stores are expected to remain an important driver of future revenue growth.

## Key Business Risks

Academy’s major risks include:

- Reduced consumer spending during economic downturns
- Competition from Dick’s Sporting Goods, Walmart, Amazon, specialty retailers, and brands selling directly to customers
- New stores failing to generate enough sales or profit
- Excess or outdated inventory
- Tariffs and internationally manufactured merchandise becoming more expensive
- Supply-chain and transportation disruptions
- Changes in customer preferences
- Theft and inventory shrink
- Seasonal fluctuations in demand
- Geographic concentration in the southern United States

## Initial Analyst Takeaway

Academy has an understandable retail business with recognizable products, established cash flow, and opportunities to grow through new stores. However, its performance depends heavily on consumer demand, inventory management, competitive pricing, and the success of its store-expansion strategy.
